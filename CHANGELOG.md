# Changelog

Each release on GitHub shows the section of its version. Add a `## X.Y.Z - date` section before tagging `vX.Y.Z`: the release workflow fails without it.

## 1.25.0 - 2026-10-23

- **Team leagues:** a new league can be created as **One card** (as before) or **👥 Teams of three**. In a team league each player fights with their Elite Four team (the battle card plus the album's Team zone), and a duel is the same series of 1v1 fights as against the Elite Four: the winner keeps its HP, the loser is replaced, and the whole series is one Elo result. The server registers the two backup cards with the fighter (same level cap; their XP counts in the anti-cheat save check), only lets full teams fight (checked before a daily duel is spent), stores the whole series and mirrors it for the defender's replay. The League tab shows each player's team and yours, the arena plays the series fight by fight with **Next duel**, and the backup cards earn half the battle XP. Existing leagues stay single-card.

## 1.24.0 - 2026-10-23

- **Balance: the cache no longer counts for the game.** Packs, coins, XP, grading and the other token-based progress now only count new input and output tokens. Tokens served from cache are left out: an agent re-reads its whole context on every round, so a few minutes of agent work could fill several packs, levels and evolutions (the quota bar and the usage figures still show the real total). Each record of usage can only raise its share, so a read that comes back short for a moment (a locked database or a half-written file) is no longer counted twice. Saves from earlier versions are moved to the new scale automatically: every bar (training, next pack, grading) keeps the progress it had. Progress already earned is not taken away, it just grows at a slower pace from now on. The pack rate (750K tokens per pack) is unchanged and will be reviewed with real use.
- **Fix: endless evolutions.** A card that already evolved and is put back to train earns levels but no longer makes another copy of its evolution (nor another graduation pack). Evolutions made before this version are not marked.

## 1.23.0 - 2026-10-23

- **Cancel grading:** a card at the grading service can now be taken back from its viewer (**✖ Cancel grading**), so it can train, fight or join the team again. After a confirmation that shows the amount, half of the fee is returned (rounded down) and the other half is lost. Cancelling does not count as a try, and the card keeps any grade it already had.
- **Sleeve:** removes the white band that was drawn across the top of sleeved cards and hid part of the art.

## 1.22.0 - 2026-10-22

- **New public ranking page** (`/l/<invite code>`): a podium with the card of each of the top three, a ranking with card art, level, grade and type chips, a win-rate bar and sorting by Elo, wins or win %; players flagged BUSTED are shown dimmed with a label; the showcase gallery has rarity-coloured frames, a shine on hover and a lightbox with the large card; the season has a progress bar with the days left, and last season's podium has its own panel; duels are grouped by day (Today, Yesterday…), show the Elo each side won or lost and a relative time, and clicking a player filters them. It also adds a copy-link button, English and Spanish by the browser language, mobile layout without horizontal scroll, loading, empty and error states, automatic refresh every minute and keyboard and screen-reader support. The page is now split into `league.html`, `league.css` and `league.js` and served with a strict Content-Security-Policy. **The league server serves it, so it goes live when `main` is deployed.**

## 1.21.0 - 2026-10-22

- **More battle scenes (14 new, 27 in total):** every gym leader now fights in a place of their own (Brock's cave, Misty's pool, Surge's electric lab, Erika's greenhouse, Koga's ninja house, Sabrina's psychic room, Blaine's lava lab, Giovanni's hall), each Elite Four trainer in their hall (Lorelei's ice cave, Bruno's dojo, Agatha's graveyard, Lance's throne room), the daily cup in a colosseum and the weekly boss in a dark arena. Rivals without a place of their own still fight in the scene of their type. The scenes are original pixel art generated by `scripts/build-scenes.mjs`. Also fixes the snow caps on the mountains of the peak scene, which were drawn black.

## 1.20.0 - 2026-10-22

- **League showcase:** each player shows off one card in their leagues: the one you pin with **⭐ Pin as showcase** in the card viewer, or otherwise your most valuable card (market value x grade x condition; "Choose automatically" goes back to that). It is always visible in the **album header** (whether or not you play online), the League tab has a **Your showcase** box that tells you if the server has it, and it appears next to your row in the League tab and in a new **Showcase** gallery on the public ranking page (`/l/<code>`), with its grade and an estimated value that the server works out from the TCGdex price. It is a claim: the server only checks that the card exists and the grade is valid, and players flagged BUSTED show none. The card is stored on the league server and removed with **Delete my online data**. **Needs the league server updated** (it is deployed from `main`).

## 1.19.0 - 2026-10-22

- **Teams of three and the Elite Four:** the battle card can now be backed up by two more cards (a new **Team** zone above the album, a button in the card viewer; a wet card or one out for grading leaves the team by itself). Once you hold all 8 gym badges (the Battle tab says so and shows how many you have), it opens the **Elite Four** (Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha, Lance), each with a team of three cards (levels 34-48). The fight is a series of 1v1 duels where the winner keeps its remaining HP and the loser is replaced by the next card. The first win over each trainer pays 🪙 1,200-2,500 and 1-3 mystery boxes, and rematches pay 🪙 10 (3 a day). The leading card earns battle XP and the backup cards half of it. The battle simulator can now start a fight with a fighter already hurt (`startHp`); fights without it, and league duels, are unchanged.

## 1.18.0 - 2026-10-22

- **League seasons:** each league now plays by calendar month. When a new month starts, the top three of the season (players who fought and are not flagged BUSTED) are archived, and everybody's Elo goes halfway back to 1000 with a clean win-loss record (also for members who left, so rejoining cannot bring back an old high). The League tab and the public ranking page show the current season, when it ends, last season's podium and a 🏆 next to each player's seasons won. Deleting your online data removes your trophies. The server archives with one guarded update, so concurrent requests cannot do it twice. **Needs the league server updated** (it is deployed from `main`).

## 1.17.0 - 2026-10-22

- **Weekly boss:** a new boss every Monday (level 40-50, the same for everybody) in the Battle tab. Three attempts a week; the first victory of the week pays 🪙 400 and a mystery box. The tab shows your odds before you fight.
- **Daily cup:** three fights in a row against rivals a bit stronger than your card, one cup a day. Entry 🪙 30; 2 wins pay 🪙 45 and 3 wins pay 🪙 120 and a mystery box. If the rivals' cards cannot be downloaded, nothing is charged.
- **Card frames:** five cosmetic frames (Gold, Neon, Flame, Frost, Rainbow; 🪙 200-800) sold in the Items tab, drawn around the thumbnails and the card viewer. A way to spend coins once everything else is bought.

## 1.16.0 - 2026-10-22

- **Clearer battles:** a super effective hit shows ▲ and a not very effective one ▼ next to the damage; a critical hit flashes the arena and gets a beat of its own; fighters walk in at the start and the loser drops when the fight ends. The HP bar is also backed by an icon (♥ healthy, ⚠ hurt, ❗ critical, ☠ fainted) and is exposed to screen readers as a progress bar, so it does not depend on telling colours apart. Keyboard focus is visible on tabs, chips, thumbnails and buttons.
- **Optional sound effects (off by default):** `tokenBinder.sound.enabled` plays short sounds when you flip a card or open a pack (rarer cards get a flourish), hit or miss in a fight, win or lose, level up and reach a goal. They are generated in the webview: there are no audio files.
- **Goals tab:** 19 long-term goals (collection size, every type, rare finds, battle wins and streaks, gym badges, completed lines, card levels, graded cards…) with a progress bar and a reward (coins, mystery boxes or packs) the first time you reach each one. The side panel shows how many you have and links to the tab. Saves from before this version record the goals they already meet without paying for them.
- **"Since last time" card:** the side panel keeps a summary of what happened (new packs, coins earned, level ups, evolutions, completed lines, graded cards, goals) until you dismiss it, instead of only showing toasts you may miss.
- **Next step:** a box at the top of the side panel points at the one thing worth doing now (open your packs, choose an evolution, fill a training slot, open a mystery box, pick a battle card, spend your coins).
- **First-use guide:** new players get a three-step welcome card. Players who already own cards do not see it.

## 1.15.0 - 2026-10-22

- **Lighter side panel:** the shop, the daily chart and the usage by model are collapsible boxes (closed by default, the shop summary shows your coins and how many items you can afford); the panel remembers which ones you opened.
- **Drag and drop in the album:** a bar above the album has a **Training** zone and a **Battle card** zone. Drag a card onto them to train it or take it to battle, and drag a card from the training zone back onto the grid to retire it. Zones turn green or red depending on whether the card can go there. The viewer buttons still work.
- **Type filter:** filter the album by Pokémon type (Fire, Water, Grass…); thumbnails show the type icon.
- **Battle backgrounds in pixel art:** each fight has its own environment drawn as retro pixel art with two standing platforms (volcano against Fire, lake against Water, forest, power plant, temple, dojo, night, factory, mountain peak, garden, meadow, a stadium for league duels and the Team Rocket hideout). They are original, generated by `scripts/build-scenes.mjs` (`npm run scenes`) and live in `media/scenes/`.
- **Attack animations by type:** fireballs, water drops, leaves, psychic orbs and shadow balls fly to the target; lightning, punches, metal sparks and stars strike it. Damage lands when the effect arrives, critical hits shake the arena and a miss flies past. Nothing moves with reduced motion enabled.

## 1.14.0 - 2026-10-22

- **Update needed for leagues:** the league server now requires the save summary that only 1.14.0 sends, so older versions can no longer field a fighter online.
- **League server, edited saves are caught (BUSTED):** the extension now reports its whole-save figures (coins, cards, items, levels, lifetime tokens) with the fighter, and the server flags a report that grew faster than real time allows since the last accepted one (the first report is judged against its own lifetime tokens, so a long honest history is never flagged). A BUSTED player is sorted to the bottom of every ranking and cannot challenge or be challenged. Two ways out: a **majority of the other members** vote them back in (in that league only), or they press **"I was a cheater and want to play again"**, which resets their local save to zero once the server has accepted it. Only growth is checked, so selling, mishaps or a reset never flag anyone. A client that does not send the report can no longer field a fighter.

- **League server, duel limit no longer bypassable:** the daily limits (10 a day, 2 per rival) were checked by counting the duels and then saving the new one, so firing many challenges at the same instant slipped past the count. They are now reserved with an atomic per-day counter (`duel_quota`), so a burst gets exactly the allowed number through.
- **League server, Elo cannot be reset by leaving:** a member's standing is kept in `member_stats` when they leave, so rejoining a league restores their Elo and record instead of starting fresh at 1000 (which let a losing player wipe their losses). Deleting your data still removes everything.

## 1.13.0 - 2026-10-22

- **Online leagues (opt-in, off by default):** a new **League** tab in the album. Create a private league, share its invite code and challenge your friends with the card you take to battle. Duels are asynchronous: the server plays the fight at once with the same rules as the Battle tab, moves the Elo of both players and your friend can watch the replay later. Limits: 10 challenges a day, 2 a day against the same player. Duels pay no coins; the card that fights earns battle XP as usual.
- **Ranking page:** `<server>/l/<invite code>` shows the Elo table and the latest duels to whoever has the code.
- **Privacy:** turning it on asks first (and names the server), then signs you in with the GitHub account VS Code already has. The server stores only your GitHub id and login, your battle card (level, grade, held item), roughly how much of your quota you used and your duel results. **Delete my online data** (League tab or command palette) removes everything. The server address can only be set by you, never by a workspace, and only `https` is accepted.
- **Fair play between friends:** the server looks the cards up in TCGdex itself, so both players fight with the same data and prices. A new player can field cards up to level 10 and the cap rises 4 levels a day (anything above is clamped).
- **Your own server:** the `server/` folder holds the league server (Vercel + Neon Postgres) and its README; the setting `tokenBinder.online.server` points the extension to it. The default one is hosted by the author.

## 1.12.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Items tab in the album:** the mystery boxes and every item you own are now shown as cards (icon, amount and what it does), grouped by use. The side panel keeps the box button and a link to the tab.
- **Album filters and sorting:** filter by condition (graded, mint, worn, soaked, sleeve or toploader) and by held item, and sort by level or by condition.
- **Condition and held item visible on the thumbnails:** a worn card shows its condition (NM, LP, MP, HP) and a card holding an item shows its icon, without opening it.
- **New slab design:** graded cards sit in a thick clear case with a label (TBGS brand, name, set and number, certificate, four subgrades and the grade in a black box; a gold-on-black label for a 10).

## 1.11.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Mishaps for going over the quota** (opt-in: turn them on with `tokenBinder.mishaps.enabled`; contributed by Hector in #1). Past 100% of your own monthly quota, one mishap per quota period: **Team Rocket** steals a spare card (60%; win it back in the Battle tab), you **trip** and drop spare copies while another 10% of the cards get scuffed or lose their sleeve (30%; graded cards are safe), or the **rain** soaks up to 6 cards (10%; sleeves and slabs keep cards dry). Cards you invested in are never lost: only virgin cards and spare copies can be taken. A soaked card is worthless and cannot train, battle, be graded, sold or transformed until a **Charmandryer** (40 coins, new in the shop) dries it; it comes back Heavily Played. The first period after turning them on is free.

## 1.10.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Combat economy:** rematch victories now pay 3 coins (up to 5 a day) instead of 20, so fighting complements the coins you earn from tokens instead of dwarfing them. First-victory rewards are unchanged.
- **Fighting trains the card:** the card that fights earns 10,000 XP for a win and 4,000 for a defeat, for the first 5 fights of each day. The result screen shows the XP and any level gained, and the Battle tab reminds you when your fighter is not in a training slot.
- **Held battle items** (found in mystery boxes, one per card, not used up): Sturdy Plate, Choice Band, Quick Claw and Lucky Charm. They change how a card fights, never how strong it is; balance tests keep each one at about 50% against the same card without it. Equip them from the card in the album.
- Item descriptions now say what they are worth in battle (Rare Candy about +1.8% strength, Lucky Egg about +4.5%, and that transformations change the card's stage and demand).

## 1.9.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Protection is now visible:** a sleeve is drawn over the card, a toploader as a rigid frame, and chips under the card name (with hover explanations) show what protects it and its condition. Album thumbnails get small 🛡️ / 📦 icons. Sleeves and toploaders never changed the stats; the item descriptions now say so.
- **Wear:** the card you take to battle can lose condition after a fight (10% with no protection, 2% with a sleeve, 4% with a toploader, 0% with both). A worn card looks worn, is worth 6% less per step and has worse grading odds; the battle screen shows the risk and tells you when a card got worn. A graded card in its slab never wears.

## 1.8.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Battles (1 vs 1):** take one card to battle and face the eight Kanto gym leaders. Fights are automatic (about 7 rounds), use the real attacks of the cards, and are explained afterwards ("why you won / lost"). Beating a leader gives a badge and coins and unlocks the next one; your record and recent fights are saved. A new **Battle** tab in the album.
- **A new strength model** keeps the many different cards comparable: training level matters most, then market demand, rarity, stage, grade and a small token-usage bonus (up to +8%); what is printed on a card only decides whether it leans tanky or hard-hitting, never how strong it is. The stats shown in the album come from the same model, so they have changed. Balance is fixed by tests that simulate thousands of fights with 320 real cards, and `npm run balance` prints the report.

## 1.7.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Protection and grading:** sleeves and toploaders (shop or mystery boxes) improve the odds when a card is sent to the "TBGS" grading service. It comes back after 500K tokens with a 1-10 grade, shown in a slab; re-submitting can only improve it. The grade multiplies the card's market value (10 → ×3), which also counts in the value of your collection.

## 1.6.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Mystery boxes** (120 coins) with 16 items: evolution stones and King's Rock, Rare Candy (+1 level), Lucky Egg (double XP for 1M tokens) and four rare transformation items. An *Items* panel shows what you own.
- **Choose the evolution:** lines that branch (Eevee has 8 species; also Gloom, Poliwhirl, Slowpoke, Kirlia, Snorunt, Cosmoem, Wurmple and Pikachu) now wait for you at the evolution point. Pick a species with the item it needs, take a free branch, or let it evolve naturally (random).
- **Transformations:** a finished card of level 20+ can become its Mega, VMAX, VSTAR or Terastal ex version when one exists; it keeps its level.

## 1.5.0 - 2026-10-21

- **Updates:** TokenBinder now checks GitHub once a day for a newer release and offers to install it (*Update now*, *Release notes*, *Skip this version*). It never installs anything without asking, verifies the downloaded file against the size and SHA-256 published by GitHub, and can be turned off with `tokenBinder.updates.check`. **Check for updates** is also available from the command palette.
- **Safer upgrades:** when the extension version changes, your progress is copied to a backup file first (the last 3 are kept; restore one with **Import progress**). Exported files now carry a format version, and a file exported by a newer TokenBinder is refused with a clear message instead of being half-read.
- If you are on 1.4.0 or older you need to run the installer once more to get this version; from then on updates arrive by themselves.
